# THE LAST ELEVATOR
**DARK FACTS ORIGINAL | Pilot narration draft | 60 seconds**

At 2:13 AM, the elevator opened by itself.

Nobody had pressed the button. Nobody was inside.

Then the display lit up: FLOOR ZERO.

The building only had twelve floors.

I stepped closer. The doors began to close.

A voice behind me whispered, "You forgot your badge."

I turned around. My reflection was still in the elevator.

It smiled first.

I don't work there anymore.
