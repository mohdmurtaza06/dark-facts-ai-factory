At 2:13 AM, the elevator opened by itself. I was the only person on night shift.

No button had been pressed. No footsteps. Just the soft little chime nobody wanted to hear.

The display blinked, went dark, then lit up again: FLOOR ZERO. Our building had twelve floors.

I checked the panel twice. Twelve buttons. Basement. Lobby. Floors one through ten. Absolutely no zero.

The doors started closing. Then a voice behind me said, "You forgot your badge."

I turned around. Nobody there. When I looked back, the elevator was still waiting.

My reflection stood inside. Same uniform. Same tired face. Except it was smiling.

I quit that morning. HR asked why. I told them the elevator had hired my replacement.
